## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the ethos of "premium independent cinema meets modern fintech." It balances the cultural intimacy and editorial curation of boutique arthouse institutions with the precision, cryptographic clarity, and operational confidence of high-scale financial software. 

### Design Philosophy
- **Aesthetic Movement:** Modern Editorial Minimalist with subtle structural brutalism via monospaced precision. Generous whitespace, refined hairlines, and architectural alignment anchor every interaction.
- **Audience & Context:** Discerning event-goers, patrons, and high-tier venue operators handling time-sensitive checkouts, reserved seating, and tier validation.
- **Emotional Impact:** Quietly authoritative, calm under pressure, tactile, and free of marketing hyperbole. The interface recedes to spotlight event curation while providing unambiguous transaction certainty.

## Colors

The palette relies on a warm canvas `#FAFAF7` paired with pristine card surfaces `#FFFFFF` and structured `#E7E5E0` hairline boundaries, preventing cold sterility while maintaining optimal typographic contrast.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary:** Deep Indigo (`#2D2A6E`), interactive hover state (`#3B3791`). Used for definitive action paths, primary CTAs, and active checkout commitments.
- **Neutral & Text:**
  - Base Background: `#FAFAF7` (warm off-white)
  - Surface Containers: `#FFFFFF`
  - Hairline Boundaries: `#E7E5E0`
  - High-Emphasis Ink: `#0F1419`
  - Muted Inks / Subtext: `#5C6570`
- **Tiers & Sectional Identity:**
  - *Couple:* Rose (`#E11D63`) on soft wash tint (`#FCE7F0`).
  - *VIP / Stage:* Warm Amber (`#B45309`) on amber wash tint (`#FEF3C7`).
  - *General Entry:* Deep Teal (`#0F766E`) on clean teal tint (`#CCFBF1`).
- **Demarcation & Compliance Indicators:**
  - *Alcoholic Zone (18+/21+):* Solid Amber circle (`#B45309`) with high-contrast label.
  - *Non-Alcoholic Zone (All Ages):* Outlined Teal circle (`#0F766E`) with interior knockout.
- **Semantic Feedback:**
  - *Success:* `#16A34A`
  - *Warning:* `#D97706`
  - *Danger:* `#DC2626`
  - *Information:* `#2563EB`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear functional boundaries across three distinct typefaces:

1. **Space Grotesk (Editorial/Display):** Used for titles, show titles, category headers, and tier names. Set with a tight tracking of `-0.02em` for architectural density.
2. **Inter (Interface & Body):** Handles running text, operational tooltips, form labels, and instructions. Set at a steady `1.5` line height for sustained legibility.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Financial & Data):** Set with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` across all transactional figures, timers, countdown clocks, order IDs, gate codes, seat numbers, and inventory allocations. This separates static descriptive text from dynamic, shifting data.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 4px base increment informs spacing intervals and structural placement.

### Layout Matrix
- **Desktop Grid:** 12-column fluid grid, max container width `1280px`, with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2.5rem` (40px) outer canvas margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `1.5rem` (24px) margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) outer margins.

### Checkout & Seating Layout Principles
- **Seat Map Canvas:** Free-panning isometric or orthographic viewport contained within bordered, neutral zones. Fixed sticky bottom checkout bar on mobile (`z-index: 50`) anchored with high-contrast summary text.
- **Two-Column Split (Desktop Checkout):** 7-column primary stage for selection/seating and 5-column fixed order breakdown rail, preserving scroll anchoring.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through delicate surface containment rather than heavy, dramatic drops. Layering mimics clean sheet stock and architectural overlays.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background `#FAFAF7`, flat, unshadowed.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a continuous `1px solid #E7E5E0` hairline outline, backed by ambient shadow `0 1px 2px rgba(15, 20, 25, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Dropdowns / Popovers):** Pure white surface, `1px solid #E7E5E0`, supported by a multi-stop ambient shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(15, 20, 25, 0.04), 0 8px 24px rgba(15, 20, 25, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` container with `20px` corner radii, framed by `1px solid rgba(15, 20, 25, 0.08)`, elevated by `0 12px 36px rgba(15, 20, 25, 0.10)`. Underlaid with a backdrop scrim: `#0F1419` at `40%` opacity with a subtle `2px` lens blur.

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced, structured hierarchy of rounded corners:

- **Inputs, Checkboxes, Buttons:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) for compact, reliable interactive precision.
- **Cards, Panels, In-line Drawers:** `14px` (`0.875rem`) to create soft, legible separation between modules.
- **Modals & Dialogs:** `20px` (`1.25rem`) to provide deliberate enclosure and focal isolation.
- **Badges, Status Tags, Counter Indicators:** `9999px` full pill radius.
- **Iconography Standard:** 20px grid bounding box with a uniform 1.75px stroke width, rounded caps, and joins to match the soft-corner geometry.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Button:** `#2D2A6E` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `8px` radius, padding `12px 20px`. Hover: `#3B3791`. Focus: `2px` offset ring in Deep Indigo.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, hairline border `1px solid #E7E5E0`, text `#0F1419`. Hover: Background `#F4F3F0`.
- **Destructive Action:** Ghost surface with `#DC2626` text, transitioning to `#FEE2E2` fill on hover.

### Badges, Section Tiers, & Chips
- **Tier Chips:** Height `28px`, `9999px` radius, `8px` horizontal padding. Font: `JetBrains Mono 12px/Medium`.
  - *VIP:* Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.2)`.
  - *Couple:* Background `#FCE7F0`, text `#E11D63`, border `1px solid rgba(225, 29, 99, 0.2)`.
  - *General:* Background `#CCFBF1`, text `#0F766E`, border `1px solid rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.2)`.
- **Zone Demarcation Markers:**
  - *Alcoholic (18+/21+):* 8px solid circle fill `#B45309` followed by label text in `#0F1419`.
  - *Non-Alcoholic (All Ages):* 8px circle with 1.75px outline stroke `#0F766E` and empty interior.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Container:** Height `44px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E7E5E0`, radius `8px`, text `Inter 14px` (`#0F1419`). Placeholder color: `#5C6570`.
- **Input Focus State:** Border color `#2D2A6E` with a subtle `3px` focus ring tinted at `12%` Indigo opacity.
- **Quantity Adjuster / Stepper:** Segmented pill with `JetBrains Mono` tabular counter flanked by hairline stroke increment/decrement touch targets.

### Event Cards & Ticket Stubs
- **Card Enclosure:** Flat surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E7E5E0`, radius `14px`, overflow hidden.
- **Ticket Stubs:** Dotted division (`1.5px` dashed `#E7E5E0`) dividing performance metadata from barcode/scanning terminal areas. All financial values, seat letters, and entry times must render strictly in `JetBrains Mono`.

### Lists & Key-Value Metadata Pairs
- **Data Rows:** Divided by hairline rule `1px solid #E7E5E0`. Label rendered in `Inter 13px` (`#5C6570`), value rendered right-aligned in `JetBrains Mono 14px` (`#0F1419`).
